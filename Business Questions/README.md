🔍 Business Questions Analyzed

This project addresses 11 practical business questions using SQL analysis on Anita’s Veg Café’s sales, menu, and loyalty-program data.

💰 Mission 01 — Customer Value & Spending

**Q1. What is the total amount spent by each customer?**

This question identifies the café’s highest-value customers by calculating the total revenue generated by each customer. The results can support VIP promotions, personalized offers, and customer-retention strategies.

**Q2. How many distinct days has each customer placed an order?**

This analysis measures visit frequency rather than simply counting the number of items purchased. A customer who visits on multiple days may demonstrate stronger engagement than someone who orders many items during a single visit.

🍽️ Mission 02 — Menu Preferences

**Q3. What was the first dish ordered by each customer?**

This question identifies the first product that each customer tried. The result can help reveal which menu items create a strong first impression and attract new customers.

**Q4. Which menu item is the most popular overall?**

This analysis determines the café’s best-selling dish by counting product orders across all customers. The result can guide menu planning, inventory management, and promotional campaigns.

**Q5. What is the most frequently ordered dish for each customer?**

This question identifies each customer’s personal favorite based on order frequency. Understanding individual preferences can help the café create targeted recommendations and personalized promotions.

🎖️ Mission 03 — Loyalty Program Impact

**Q6. What dish did each loyalty member order first after joining the program?**

This analysis examines the first purchase made after loyalty enrollment. It helps measure how customers behave immediately after joining the program and which products they choose during this stage.

**Q7. What was each customer’s last order before joining the loyalty program?**

This question captures the customer’s final pre-loyalty purchase. It provides a reference point for comparing behavior before and after loyalty membership.

**Q8. How many items did each member purchase, and how much did they spend before joining?**

This analysis creates a pre-loyalty baseline by measuring both the number of items purchased and the total amount spent. The baseline is useful for evaluating whether loyalty membership changes customer activity.

⭐ Mission 04 — Rewards & Churn Detection

**Q9. How many loyalty points did each customer earn?**

Customers earn 10 points for every ₹1 spent, while Paneer Butter Masala earns double points. This question applies conditional reward logic to convert customer spending into loyalty points.

**Q10. How many points did Aarav and Meera earn during their first loyalty week?**

During the first seven days after joining the loyalty program, members earn double points on all products. This time-based analysis evaluates the impact of the introductory reward period for Aarav and Meera.

**Q11. Which customers have not placed an order in the last 30 days?**

This question identifies customers who may be at risk of churn by checking whether their most recent order is more than 30 days old. The café can use this information to send reminders, special offers, or re-engagement campaigns.

📌 Business Impact

Together, these 11 questions help Anita’s Veg Café understand customer value, visit frequency, product popularity, individual preferences, loyalty behavior, reward effectiveness, and churn risk. The analysis converts raw transaction data into insights that can support revenue growth, menu optimization, customer retention, and smarter marketing decisions.
